# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Discuss the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Say what is wrong and why, with enough detail that it can be acted on. "This is bad" is not a review.
- Accept that you may be the one who is wrong, and say so plainly when you are.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language.

## What is not tolerated

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of a sexual nature.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and any other space where the project is being worked on.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **global.inovacao@grpotencial.com.br**. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared further without your agreement.

The maintainers will remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this document, and may ban a contributor for behaviour they judge harmful. Where a report is unclear or contested, the maintainers will say what they decided and why.
